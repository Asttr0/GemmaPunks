# Demo script

1. Sign in as the synthetic Berrechid grocery merchant.
2. Upload the known purchase receipt.
3. Add today’s Darija/French sales voice note.
4. Open the agent timeline while Gemma creates a draft.
5. Answer: “Yes, the sugar quantity is 10 bags.”
6. Confirm the draft; point out that no record changed before this click.
7. Show updated sales, profit, and inventory.
8. Open the four-day cooking-oil stockout warning.
9. Compare the three seeded supplier offers.
10. Show why the 18.50 MAD offer cannot be used for 20 units alone.
11. Reveal 35 compatible, anonymized units from two nearby merchants.
12. Review the 55-unit collective proposal.
13. Show 70 MAD product saving + 30 MAD delivery saving = 100 MAD.
14. Join, then explicitly approve participation.
15. Switch to the supplier portal and show the consolidated opportunity.
16. Close on the impact panel and the line:

> One small shop has little negotiating power. A network of small shops has a
> market.

## Recovery cues

- AI timeout: switch `AI_PROVIDER=fixture`; explain that calculations and
  approvals remain unchanged.
- Dirty data: run the project-safe reset command from issue #19.
- Network failure: use the offline video and final-state screenshots.
